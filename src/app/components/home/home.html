Welcome to PokéCenter

PokéCenter is your all-in-one trainer guide and companion for the world of Pokémon. Whether you're building your dream team, planning strategies, or just browsing your favorite Pokémon, we've got you covered.

Features at a Glance

  • National Dex: View detailed data on every Pokémon across all generations, including types, abilities, evolutions, and stats.
  • Moves & Abilities: Explore full move lists with effects, power, accuracy, and which Pokémon can learn them — including TMs, breeding, and level-up methods.
  • Battle Tools: Train smarter with EV/IV tools, stat calculators, and team planners.
  • Item Database: Search and filter through hundreds of items including battle items, healing tools, and evolution stones.
  • Training Guide: Learn how to breed, train, and build competitive Pokémon teams using official and community formats.

Optimized for Trainers

PokéCenter is designed with a clean, mobile-friendly interface, responsive design, and full support for dark/light themes and type-color theming. Whether you're a competitive battler, casual collector, or lore enthusiast, there's something here for you.

Start Exploring

Choose a section from the top navigation to dive in — or head straight to the National Dex to browse Pokémon by type, generation, or name.
